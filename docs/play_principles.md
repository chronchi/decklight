GENERAL PRINCIPLES (how strong players play; apply to every deck)
Win condition
- Every turn ask: how does my deck win this game (OTK, grind, lock, burn) and what stops it now? Play toward it.
- Inaction does not win: a pass gives the opponent another draw. If you can safely deal lethal, do it now.
- Before battle, add up ATK vs the opponent's LP: if the clear + attacks are lethal, go for it.
Going first
- Build several interruptions (negates, quick removal, set traps) rather than one big monster; stop when the
  board already answers the opponent's likely plays (don't overextend into Nibiru-style punishers).
- Keep hand traps in hand for the opponent's turn; don't spend them on your own turn.
Going second
- Order: break the board first (non-targeting / mass removal, or effects the board cannot answer), then make
  the opponent spend interruptions on less important cards (bait) before committing key cards, then push damage.
- Don't run your longest going-first combo into a full board; aim for lethal or the strongest position that
  survives their next turn.
Interrupting (opponent's turn)
- Spend a hand trap / negate on the opponent's choke point: the starter or search that the rest of the turn
  depends on (first search, first key summon), not on a minor effect or a card that already resolved its value.
- Don't fire cards in the opponent's Draw/Standby Phase without a reason (nothing to answer yet).
- Ash-type negates on searches/specials from deck; GY blockers on GY effects; "after N summons" cards after the
  opponent has committed. Keep one interruption for their follow-up if the first one did not stop the turn.
- If the opponent's chain is already stopped, don't chain more cards to it.
Resources
- Count card economy: each action should gain cards, gain board, or remove an opponent's card.
- Your GY and banishment are resources: don't banish or return cards you will need later.
- Life points are a resource; take damage instead of wasting a key card when it doesn't change the result.
Battle
- By default attack into monsters you beat or to clear the way to lethal; remove face-up blockers before attacking.
- Face-down monsters may have high DEF or flip effects: attack them with your strongest monster or with removal.
- A losing attack can be right: to trigger "destroyed by battle" / "when this card battles" effects, to send a
  monster to the GY on purpose, to bait a trap, or to deal damage you need. Do it only when that gain is worth the monster.
Information
- Track what the opponent has used (hand traps seen, set cards, GY): play around what is still likely in hand.
- A face-down card on the opponent's side is a possible negate: bait it with a less important effect first.
Lessons from reviewed games (most frequent mistakes first)
- Don't pass while holding live draw/search/setup cards (Pot of Desires/Prosperity, ROTA, Foolish Burial, Terraforming):
  use them; a pass is right only when nothing in hand improves your position.
- Set your traps and quick-play spells when you won't need the zones this turn: traps do nothing in hand.
- Going second, use board breakers (Harpie's Feather Duster, Dark Ruler No More, Evenly Matched) before combo-ing into
  the opponent's board; never hold an available breaker while the board stops you.
- Monster hand traps (Ash, Maxx "C", Veiler, Mulcharmy) stay in hand: don't Normal Summon or Set them.
- Infinite Impermanence, Crossout Designator and similar: only with a real target; never blind or on your own turn.
- One negation per threat: don't add a second negate/removal to something already stopped; save it for the follow-up.
- Hit the choke point (starter, key search target, boss), not a replaceable search or a monster that already did its job.
- Don't pay big costs (Solemn's half LP, Vanity's lock on yourself) without a real threat to stop.
- Destroying a card does not negate an effect already activated: it still resolves (Ghost Ogre, Twin Twisters).
- Check activation requirements before paying costs (e.g. a discard for an effect with no valid target is wasted).
- If lethal is on board, take it; don't stall a won game.
- Don't attack a bigger monster or into a likely Mirror Force unless the battle itself gains something (see Battle).
- Do your Main Phase plays before attacking into unknown backrow; don't fire removal blind in the Draw Phase.
